About

I am a PhD student in the joint doctoral program between the Beijing Institute of Mathematical Sciences and Applications (BIMSA) and Renmin University of China, in the group Theory and Computation of PDE.

My research sits at the intersection of applied mathematics, scientific computing and machine learning: multiscale theory, the forward and inverse problems of differential equations, and learning-based methods for solving and inferring across scales.

Research interests

Multiscale methods · Partial and ordinary differential equations · Inverse problems · Scientific machine learning · Model reduction · Knowledge distillation
